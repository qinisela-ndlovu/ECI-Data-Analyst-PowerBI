# ECI-Data-Analyst-PowerBI
Analysis of Economic Complexity Index (ECI) trends and influencing factors in Sub-Saharan Africa (1995–2018) using Power BI.

## Project Overview
This project analyses the Economic Complexity Index(ECI) of Sub-Saharan African countries between 1995 and 2018 using Powe BI. The goal is to identifty the factors that influence a country's ECI, examine how ECI has changed over time and explore its geographical distribution across the region.

The project was completed as part of a data visualision and business intelligence assignment, with the final output presented as an  interactive Power BI dasbboard.

## Objectives

- Analyse ECI trends over time.
- Identify variables associated with ECI.
- Compare economic complexity between countries.
- Visualise regional patterns using interactive dashboards.

## The Dataset

## Key Findings

- GDP Growth
GDP per capita shows a positive relationship with Economic Complexity Index (ECI), despite differences in economic growth rates across countries.
North African countries generally perform better, while Central Africa tends to lag behind.

- ECONOMIC COMPLEXITY
There is considerable variation in the composition of regional economies. Countries with better infrastructure tend to exhibit higher levels of economic complexity, suggesting a positive relationship between infrastructure and ECI.
Eswatini has the highest average ECI followed by SA, these are also two of the most diversified economies on the continent • There is a significant proportion of African countries with low economic complexity with ECI values clustered around -1 (40%).

- CORRELATED ECONOMIC MEASURES 
Connectivity variables are important, as tourism arrivals an air transport activity increase, the data shows a positive association with increasing ECI. 
ECI is positively associated with CO₂ emissions. Across the study period, CO₂ emissions increased at a faster rate than GDP in many countries, regardless of their level of economic sophistication
